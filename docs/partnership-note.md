**Subject: Partnering on DUM360 — a sovereign national compute mesh from idle lab hardware**

Dear Professor [Name],

I'm building **DUM360 (Distributed Unified Mesh 360)** — an effort to pool India's
**idle computing power** into a single secure, sovereign network hosted entirely on
Indian infrastructure. At any moment most of the nation's compute sits unused:
university and research labs overnight, on weekends and vacations; consumer PCs and
phones after hours; PSU and enterprise machines outside business hours. DUM360
harvests this already-paid-for capacity for **affordable AI inference and research
compute** in normal times, and as a **strategic national reserve** for emergencies —
all consistent with the DPDP Act.

**Why universities matter most.** Campus labs are the highest-value tier: wired,
powered, predictable, and cross-connected over the **National Knowledge Network
(NKN)** — so they can sustain real parallel workloads that consumer devices cannot.
A grounded estimate (sources attached): a few hundred institutes contributing idle
overnight GPUs could deliver **a few exaFLOP/s of usable AI-inference throughput —
one to two orders of magnitude beyond India's *entire* current public
supercomputing capacity — without building a single new data centre.** I'm careful
to be honest here: this network is built for inference and parallel workloads, not
frontier model *training*, which consumer networks physically cannot support.

**I'd like to invite [University] to join as a founding partner**, contributing in
three ways:

1. **Idle lab compute** — opt-in overnight/weekend windows with graceful eviction
   (no disruption to daytime teaching), settled as compute-grants, credits or
   revenue-share plus electricity reimbursement.
2. **Student contribution** — a live national systems project: distributed
   scheduling, WASM/K3s edge agents, secure enclaves, verifiable computation.
3. **Research collaboration** — co-authored work on sovereign and green distributed
   computing.

A concrete first step would be a **no-risk pilot on one or two labs** plus a small
student/faculty team, under a simple MoU on sovereignty, security and settlement.

Could we find 20 minutes for a call? I'd be glad to walk through the architecture.
