## 1. The short version

- **We keep tracking to a minimum.**
- **No analytics and no non-essential cookies load until you give consent** via the banner shown on the Site.
- **You can decline analytics** and still browse the Site and join the waitlist normally.
- **We never sell your data**, and we don't put your name or email into analytics.

---

## 2. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies (local storage, pixels) work the same way. They can remember settings or help measure how a site is used.

We group them into two kinds: **strictly necessary** and **analytics (optional)**.

---

## 3. Strictly necessary (always on)

These are required for the Site to work safely and cannot be switched off. They do **not** track you for advertising. They include:

- **Remembering your consent choice** — so we don't ask you on every page.
- **Security / anti-abuse** — to detect spam or automated bot submissions on the sign-up form (we use **Firebase App Check**, with reCAPTCHA).

---

## 4. Analytics (only with your consent)

If — and only if — you **accept** in the consent banner, we use **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to understand, in aggregate, how the Site is used: which sections people read, where sign-ups come from, and how the sign-up flow performs. This helps us improve the Site.

How we limit it:

- **Consent-gated:** GA4 does **not** load or set analytics cookies until you consent.
- **IP anonymisation** is enabled.
- **No personal data in analytics:** we do **not** send your name, email, phone, or message into analytics events.
- We track aggregate, non-identifying events (for example: a CTA was clicked, the sign-up form was opened, a sign-up was submitted/succeeded, and how far down the page visitors scroll).

GA4 is provided by **Google**. Your use of it is also subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

The Site is also hosted on **Firebase Hosting** (a global CDN) and stores sign-up data in **Cloud Firestore (Mumbai, India)** — see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for what that means for your data.
