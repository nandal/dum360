What we collect (waitlist)

When you join the DUM360 waitlist we collect only what's needed to follow up:

  • Audience type — e.g. citizen, student, university, government/PSU, solar operator, enterprise/MSME, AI client, developer.
  • Email — required; our primary contact and de-duplication key.
  • Name — optional.
  • Organisation — for institutional/operator/enterprise/AI-client signups.
  • Phone — optional (citizen providers who prefer WhatsApp/call follow-up).
  • A short message / workload description — optional.
  • Technical/source signals — referrer, UTM parameters, landing path, and a coarse non-identifying user-agent hash for abuse prevention.

Where your data is stored

Waitlist data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the Mumbai region (asia-south1), India. This marketing website itself is served from a global content-delivery network (Firebase Hosting); the static pages contain no personal data. The “100% Indian-hosted” claim elsewhere on this site refers to the DUM360 compute platform and its operational data, which run on Indian clouds — see the home page footer note.

Why we use it & purpose limitation

We use your data only to contact you about DUM360 (early access, pilots, partnerships) for your selected segment. We do not sell or share it. Consent is explicit (an un-pre-ticked checkbox at sign-up).

Your rights (DPDP-aligned)

You can ask us to view, correct, or delete your data at any time. If you create an account (optional Google sign-in), you can manage or withdraw your entry yourself in the contributor portal. To exercise any right, email privacy@dum360.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@dum360.com.
