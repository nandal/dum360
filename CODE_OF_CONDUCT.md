# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are building national infrastructure — distributed compute for India's sovereignty. This demands seriousness, honesty, and respect. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, education, socio-economic status, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior
- Be honest about what the platform can and cannot do. Never overclaim.
- Communicate clearly and professionally. Assume good faith.
- Give constructive, specific feedback. Accept it gracefully.
- Respect that this is a long-term national project, not a quick win.
- Credit others' work. Don't claim contributions you didn't make.

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks in any form.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting misleading benchmarks or false claims.
- Spamming issues, PRs, or discussions.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to [hello@dum360.com](mailto:hello@dum360.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Honesty Addendum (DUM360-Specific)

Given DUM360's role as national infrastructure, these additional standards apply:

1. **No fabricated benchmarks.** Any performance claim must be reproducible and consistent with [docs/compute-benchmark.md](docs/compute-benchmark.md).
2. **No false partnerships.** Do not imply endorsements or partnerships that don't exist.
3. **Sovereignty precision.** Claims about data residency must be precise about which component is Indian-hosted.
4. **Emergency-mode honesty.** Rashtra Seva is a designed protocol — do not imply it is operational today.
5. **Report honestly.** If you find a security vulnerability or a factual error in public claims, report it. Don't sit on it.
