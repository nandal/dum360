How it works

From idle device to national compute

Enroll once, set your hours, and let DUM360 safely put your spare cycles to work — on your terms.

1

Install & verify

Citizens, campuses, enterprises and PSUs install a lightweight sandboxed node (K3s/KubeEdge) or mobile WASM client. Hardware integrity is verified via Secure Enclave.

2

Pre-inform availability

Book recurring windows — e.g. 11 PM–7 AM. The orchestrator maps workloads hours in advance.

3

Compute safely

Jobs run inside encrypted, confidential sandboxes you can't read — and we never read your files. Tasks mirror across ≥3 machines for resilience.

4

Get paid

Usage is metered to the rupee — paid straight to your UPI VPA, or as credits/grants for institutions. Graceful drain returns your device 30 min before your exit.

You stay in control — pause, edit your hours, or uninstall anytime. Your device is always yours.

AI inference, made simple

How big models actually run on the mesh

DUM360 matches model size to interconnect quality — so inference scales without pretending home broadband is a supercomputer fabric.

⚡

Small models → whole requests to whole nodes

Models that fit one node (7B–34B quantized) are replicated; whole requests route to whole nodes — scaling to millions of concurrent inferences nationwide.

🧩

Big models → split inside one cluster

Genuinely large models (70B–400B+) are split only inside well-connected clusters — a campus lab, a GPU rig, a PSU data centre — where bandwidth and latency can sustain it.

🌐

Biggest models → pipeline across campuses

For the biggest models, pipeline parallelism spans institutions over the National Knowledge Network — a loosely-coupled academic grid, not consumer broadband.

Built for massively-parallel, latency-tolerant work — inference, rendering, simulation. Not for training frontier models across home internet — the physics doesn't allow it, and we won't pretend it does. Plus speculative decoding: small models draft tokens, big cluster-hosted models verify, to stretch scarce big-model capacity. Read the honest comparison →
